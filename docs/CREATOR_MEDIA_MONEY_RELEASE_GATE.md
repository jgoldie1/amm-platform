## Aniyah Cross-Border Payments
This is a platform/orchestration layer, not a claim that TRYAMM is itself a licensed bank, money transmitter, FX dealer or remittance provider.

Required production controls:
- regulated payment/payout partner per supported country;
- KYC/KYB and sanctions screening where required;
- beneficiary validation and payout consent;
- currency/FX quote disclosure before authorization;
- platform/creator/provider/tax/processor fee separation;
- immutable Money Engine ledger and reconciliation;
- payout status, failure, refund and dispute handling;
- country/currency/rail capability matrix;
- transaction and velocity limits;
- fraud/risk review;
- audit evidence and provider webhook verification;
- no unsupported country, currency or payment rail may silently fall back to a different flow.

## Financial Literacy
Creator earnings screens should explain gross revenue, platform fees, processor fees, refunds/chargebacks, taxes/withholding when applicable, net earnings, available balance, pending balance and payout history. Education must be clearly distinguished from individualized financial, tax, investment or legal advice.

Creator tools should include budgeting, savings-goal planning, revenue/expense categorization, royalty statements, payout explanations, scam/fraud warnings and links to qualified professionals where regulated advice is needed.

## LIVE technical GREEN gate
LIVE remains YELLOW until a real production-like two-device test passes:
1. Device A signs in.
2. A creates a room and receives a valid publisher token.
3. A publishes camera + microphone.
4. Presence becomes LIVE.
5. Device B sees A in discovery.
6. B receives a valid subscriber token and joins.
7. B receives audio/video.
8. Realtime chat works both directions.
9. Gift transaction is authorized and ledgered exactly once.
10. PK invitation/accept/state synchronization works.
11. Disconnect/reconnect succeeds without duplicate gift/order state.
12. Host ends room.
13. Recording finalizes when enabled.
14. Replay metadata persists.
15. Authorized clip/reel can be generated and published.
16. Moderation and rights gates are enforced.

## Rights GREEN gate
Technical playback does not imply distribution rights. A media item is rights-GREEN only when the required master/recording, composition/publishing, synchronization, audiovisual/performance and/or distribution rights for the requested use and territory are verified as applicable. Expired, unknown or blocked territories fail closed.

## Media technical GREEN gate
A media item is technical-GREEN only after upload/storage integrity, malware/media validation, successful transcode, playable adaptive rendition, metadata persistence, accessibility assets where required, CDN delivery and client playback tests pass.

## Payment GREEN gate
Cross-border/payment features become GREEN per country + currency + rail only after the real regulated provider is configured and sandbox/live-mode test evidence proves authorization, webhook verification, ledger posting, payout/reversal behavior and reconciliation. Never mark an entire global payment system GREEN from one successful country test.

## Release states
- GREEN: real end-to-end evidence exists for the exact feature/territory/provider path.
- YELLOW: visible and safely gated; missing provider, credential, rights or test evidence.
- RED: hidden/disabled because use could create security, money, rights, safety or compliance risk.

## Global release matrix
Track technical streaming, media rights, payments/payouts, Free TV distribution, moderation/localization and accessibility independently by territory. China, Russia, Nigeria, South Africa, United States and every other market require their own evidence rather than a universal global switch.

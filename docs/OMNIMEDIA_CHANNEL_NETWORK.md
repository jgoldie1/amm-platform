# TRYAMM OmniMedia Channel Network

## Purpose
Unify creator media, news, debate, faith, education, sports, music, Free TV, Isaiah AI TV, StarVerse, podcasts and community broadcasting under one rights-aware, accessible, multilingual distribution system.

## Channel lanes
- TRYAMM News: live reporting, interviews, explainers, local/community news and creator journalism.
- Debate Arena: scheduled moderated debates, multi-person panels, audience questions, fact/source cards, timed rounds and replay clips.
- Faith Channels: churches, ministries, Sabbath/New Moon programming, scripture study, music, teaching and community broadcasts. Faith lanes are user-selectable and do not receive privileged ranking over other beliefs.
- Free TV / Omni Box: authorized FAST-style linear channels, creator channels, short dramas, movies and licensed programming.
- Isaiah AI TV: creator programming, sports/youth-safe lanes, AI-assisted show planning and distribution.
- HoloMusic: audio streaming, music videos, premieres, playlists, artist channels and royalty reporting.
- StarVerse: auditions, talent competitions, casting, performances and fan discovery.
- Podcasts / Talk: audio/video shows, call-ins, panels and clips.
- Sports / Games / Living Worlds: authorized events, esports, game streams and interactive programming.
- Education / Financial Literacy: courses, explainers and creator financial-literacy programming with regulated-advice boundaries.
- Community / Public Interest: local events, emergency/public-service information and community creators.

## News integrity controls
News channels need source/provenance fields, correction/version history, visible opinion/commentary labels, synthetic/AI-media disclosure, impersonation/deepfake controls, election/civic safety rules where applicable, and moderation/escalation. TRYAMM must not present AI-generated material as independently verified reporting.

## Debate controls
Debates support host/moderator roles, speaker queue, timed rounds, mute/remove controls, audience question queue, source cards, captions/translation, safety moderation and replay chapters. Ranking must not secretly favor a political viewpoint.

## Faith controls
Faith broadcasters receive the same creator, LIVE, donation/commerce, accessibility, localization and media tooling subject to law and payment/provider rules. Religious identity and private faith activity must not be exposed through public presence without user choice.

## Global distribution
Every program/channel carries territory, language, age-rating, rights window, monetization mode and accessibility metadata. Availability in the US, Nigeria, South Africa, China, Russia or any other market is independently gated by rights, provider availability, applicable law/regulation, sanctions/export controls when relevant, and platform safety requirements.

## Accessibility baseline
Captions, transcripts, keyboard/switch access, screen-reader semantics, high contrast, scalable type, reduced-motion support, audio description metadata where available, translation, readable chat and accessible moderation/reporting controls are release requirements rather than optional channel features.

## GREEN evidence
A channel is not globally GREEN because its UI exists. Technical streaming, rights, moderation, payments, accessibility/localization and territory availability each have separate evidence gates. LIVE requires a real two-device publish/discover/join/audio/video/chat/gift/PK/reconnect/end/record/replay/clip test. Free TV/music/news distribution additionally requires rights/provenance and playback evidence for the exact territory.

# TRYAMM Holographic Time + Mission Worlds

## Recovered foundation
This specification restores the recovered Living Worlds concepts into the active persistent-universe branch rather than treating them as new disconnected apps.

Recovered archive evidence includes:
- HoloPresence Transference: a volumetric/AR holographic twin transmitted to another approved destination, not physical teleportation.
- Echo Travelers: users enter historical simulations and possible futures without claiming literal time travel.
- Quantum Leap / Echo system: each jump can preserve an interactive record of decisions, conversations, world events and consequences for later replay/education/what-if simulation.
- Living Worlds mission system: active objectives, GPS/world coordinates, holographic HUD/rewards, portal networks and persistent mission completion.
- Planetary holographic runtime: world portals, holographic shaders and mission overlays.

## Product goal
Create a fully immersive holographic world-and-time experience that exceeds a conventional planet-exploration menu by combining persistent identity, avatar embodiment, spatial presence, missions, creator tools, educational reconstruction, fictional timelines, LIVE participation, HoloForge assets and cross-device AR/VR/MR/holographic presentation.

## Holographic presentation
Every compatible world may expose multiple presentation modes:
- Screen/TV 3D
- phone/tablet AR
- VR
- mixed reality
- spatial/holographic display adapters
- HoloPresence remote twin
- accessibility/non-XR mode

Holographic mode can render portal rings, volumetric avatars, mission HUD, navigation arrows, translated captions, spatial labels, interactive GLB objects, Lottie effects, world maps, AI guides, stage/media surfaces and remote LIVE participants. It must not claim free-space physical holography on hardware that only provides AR/2D/3D presentation.

Each destination can support:

- rover/robot/drone missions
- spacecraft navigation

## Mission to Mars vertical slice
Launch/mission briefing → crew/avatar loadout → spacecraft/cockpit experience → navigation/travel sequence → Mars orbital approach → atmospheric entry/landing simulation → surface habitat → rover/drone exploration → resource/science objectives → emergency/rescue events → build/repair tasks → LIVE mission broadcast → record highlights/reels/movie → persistent mission checkpoint → return/continue later.

Scientific simulation values and fictional game mechanics are labeled separately. Real mission/navigation data can be incorporated only from authoritative/licensed sources and must not be presented as real spacecraft control.

## Time Machine / Echo Travel
The Time Machine is an immersive simulation and narrative engine, not a claim of physical time travel.

Destination key = WORLD + TIME/TIMELINE + INSTANCE + REVISION.

Modes:
- historical reconstruction
- evidence-based educational simulation
- creator-authored historical drama
- scientifically modeled past/future
- fictional alternate history
- speculative future
- saved Echo replay

Every experience displays its evidence mode: DOCUMENTED / RECONSTRUCTED / INTERPRETIVE / FICTIONAL / SPECULATIVE.

## Slavery-history experiences
Historical slavery experiences are education/history worlds, not casual spectacle. They should be built with historians, primary/secondary sources, descendant/community consultation where appropriate, age/content guidance, trauma-aware presentation and accessibility.

Possible experiences include African societies before enslavement, transatlantic slave trade context, Middle Passage education, plantation/slave-labor systems, resistance and rebellions, maroon communities, family/community life, abolition movements, Underground Railroad, emancipation, Reconstruction and enduring legacies.

Do not gamify victim suffering for points, force users to role-play abuse, trivialize sexual violence, or turn atrocities into purchasable entertainment boosts. Missions should emphasize evidence discovery, preservation, resistance history, family/community stories, escape/rescue where historically contextualized, abolition, civic learning and restoration of names/stories.

Historical claims should carry citations/source cards in the experience. Fictionalized characters/events must be labeled.

## Yashua/Yasheau-era world
Create a first-century Judea/Galilee historical-and-faith world for exploring the social, geographic, linguistic, political and religious setting associated with Yashua/Yasheau/Jesus traditions.

Separate layers:
1. HISTORICAL CONTEXT — archaeology, geography, Roman rule, Second Temple Judaism, daily life, languages, roads, villages and historically supportable context.
2. TEXT/TRADITION — scenes tied to identified scriptural/textual traditions with source labels.
3. FAITH EXPERIENCE — devotional/interpretive presentations clearly identified as faith interpretation.
4. CREATOR STORY — fictional dramatization clearly labeled as such.

Do not make AI-generated dialogue appear to be a newly discovered quotation from a historical/religious figure. Users may explore reconstructions and textual scenes, but generated speech is labeled dramatization unless directly tied to an identified source.

Potential locations include Jerusalem, Galilee, Nazareth, Capernaum and other historically relevant locations as evidence/assets permit. The world can support guided walks, maps, architecture, markets, teaching-context scenes, music/soundscape, language overlays, source cards, faith channels and group study/LIVE sessions.

## Planet Recall successor design
The recovered platform already had Living Worlds, HoloPresence, portals, missions, persistent Echoes and holographic shaders. The successor should therefore differentiate through continuity rather than merely larger graphics:

avatar enters portal → world materializes holographically → spatial guide briefs mission → multiplayer crew joins → objects respond physically → world remembers completed objectives → Time Machine changes the same location by timeline → source/evidence layer explains reconstructed history → creator can record/publish authorized experience → another device can resume the same checkpoint.

## HoloForge integration
Worlds are assembled from stable Asset IDs. HoloForge can create authorized terrain props, habitats, clothing, historical reconstruction assets, spacecraft, rovers, buildings, characters, stages and effects. Every asset carries provenance, rights, world/timeline compatibility, quality variants and moderation state.

## AI characters
AI characters may act as guides, fictional NPCs, mission controllers and educational assistants. Historical-person simulations require clear synthetic/dramatization labels and source boundaries. AI must not invent historical claims and present them as established evidence.

## Persistence
Persist mission checkpoints, authorized inventory/entitlements, avatar state, world revision, completed evidence/source cards, creator projects and achievements. High-frequency physics/player movement stays in realtime/world servers; durable checkpoints go to authoritative storage.

## Accessibility
Every immersive mission must have a non-VR path and support applicable seated mode, one-hand controls, voice control, controller remapping, captions, transcripts, audio description, reduced motion, teleport locomotion, contrast/text sizing, translation and simplified interaction modes.

## Security
World entry, HoloPresence, device/haptic control, purchases and publishing consult Quantum Security. Panic Mode can isolate a compromised world/session/device, freeze money and privileged operations, stop haptic/device commands, quarantine uploads, preserve evidence and recover from a known-good checkpoint without destroying unrelated world progress.

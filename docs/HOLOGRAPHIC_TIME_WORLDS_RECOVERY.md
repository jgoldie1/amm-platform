# TRYAMM Holographic Time Worlds — Recovered Architecture

## Recovered from the design vault
This specification reconnects the previously designed Quantum Leap Engine concepts to the Persistent Creator Universe.

Recovered mechanics include:
- Quantum Transition Engine: save player state → dispose/stream changed assets → restore player state → continuous transition.
- Wormhole Portal System: plasma ring, particles, lens distortion, event horizon, volumetric light and spatial audio.
- Quantum Memory Stream: inventory, quests, NPC relationships, weather, time of day, AI conversations and world state persist while only changed environment data streams.
- Holographic Twin Worlds: multiple simulated eras/worlds can occupy the same virtual/geospatial coordinates and transform around the player.
- Plasma Gateway and HoloPresence-style reconstruction effects.
- Quantum AI Guide using Stubbs AI/Middleverse AI for navigation, mission explanation and transition status.
- Echo system: each jump can preserve an interactive record of decisions, conversations, events, quests and world consequences.
- Existing dynamic MissionSystem/Mission HUD concepts for objectives, coordinates, portal-linked missions and rewards.

These are simulation, storytelling, education and world-streaming systems. They do not claim physical teleportation or literal time travel.

## Holographic-first experience
Every compatible destination can expose five presentation tiers:
1. Screen/2D fallback.
2. 3D/WebGL/WebGPU world.
3. Mobile AR spatial overlay.
4. VR/MR immersive world.
5. Holo5DX / future calibrated multi-view holographic display profile.

A single stable World/Asset ID resolves the appropriate rendition. Holographic presentation uses multi-view camera rigs, motion parallax, spatial audio and device calibration where hardware supports it. Never claim physically calibrated holography until a hardware profile passes measured ghosting/crosstalk, brightness, viewing-angle, optical-center and distortion tests.

## Mars mission loop
Briefing → choose avatar/crew/vehicle → equipment and accessibility configuration → launch/cinematic or portal handoff → orbital/landing sequence → Mars world instance → mission objectives → rover/robot/drone support → science/exploration/build/rescue missions → LIVE/team communication → checkpoint/Echo → rewards/creator assets → return or continue settlement storyline.

Mission templates can use the recovered MissionSystem pattern: objective ID, world/coordinates, status, requirements, reward, completion evidence and checkpoint effects. Scientific missions must distinguish sourced/model-based facts from fictional gameplay.

## Omniverse After Dark time worlds
Omniverse After Dark may use the same Time Machine, Quantum Leap, avatar, LIVE, creator-economy, haptic and persistent-world infrastructure for consenting adults in eligible jurisdictions. Mature relationship/intimacy experiences remain age-gated, consent-driven and non-public by default, with recording/sharing requiring separate explicit consent. The system must not permit minors, coercive experiences, non-consensual likeness use, hidden recording, or a mature-world entitlement to bypass payment-provider, app-store, territory, moderation, privacy or safety restrictions.

Adult-world state is compartmentalized from general discovery. Entering an eligible mature world requires adult eligibility + territory/provider policy + account security + world entitlement + explicit session consent. Haptic/device permissions are scoped per session, have an emergency stop, expire automatically and never grant wallet, identity, memory or administrative access.

Time travel in After Dark is fictional/simulated world travel. It can place adult avatars in creator-authored fantasy/future settings, but historical/religious educational worlds remain separately governed and do not inherit sexualized gameplay merely because the same Time Machine runtime is used.

## Sheep Planet / North-Star celestial lane
Sheep Planet is a creator-authored fictional world connected to the stellar graph rather than presented as a discovered astronomical planet. It can use pastoral landscapes, celestial shepherd themes, flocks, settlements, missions, music, creator stages and faith/storytelling experiences.

The North-Star/Polaris experience is an astronomy/navigation destination. Constellations are rendered from Earth-view patterns and can expand into 3D stellar positions; the app must not imply that constellation stars are physically adjacent. Creator mythology/faith/story layers are clearly separated from observational astronomy.

## Historical Time Worlds
Historical experiences are educational/reconstructed simulations with source/provenance metadata and visible uncertainty labels. Do not present AI-generated reconstruction as direct historical footage.

### Slavery and African-diaspora history
This lane may cover African civilizations and trade networks, the transatlantic slave trade, enslavement, resistance, family/community life, abolition, emancipation and later historical consequences. It must avoid turning human suffering into trivialized spectacle or reward-driven reenactment. Use age/content guidance, contextual narration, primary/credible-source citations where available, educator/historian review, respectful avatar/NPC treatment and opt-out/skip controls.

Players can explore places, documents, oral-history-style educational narratives, economic systems, resistance networks and consequences without gamifying abuse against enslaved people.

### Yashua/Yeshua-era world
Create an ancient Judea/Jerusalem/Galilee historical-faith world that lets users explore geography, architecture, languages, occupations, travel, markets, worship contexts and events associated with the period in which Yeshua/Jesus is traditionally understood to have lived.

Separate layers must be visible:
- historical/archaeological context;
- scriptural/traditional narrative;
- creator/faith interpretation;
- fictional dramatization.

The system must not falsely claim that generated dialogue, appearance, exact locations or scenes are verified historical recordings. Faith communities can publish their own authorized interpretive experiences with clear attribution.

## Quantum Leap / Echo gameplay
Every transition can leave an Echo containing authorized world-state deltas: player decisions, restored/damaged objects, conversations, community votes, weather events, concerts, battles, marketplace events and quest consequences.

Echoes support historical education, what-if simulations, family-memory experiences, sports/concert replay worlds, training and alternate-future storytelling. Users can revisit or branch a simulated Echo without rewriting real history.

## Planetary holographic command table
The Holo5DX/Twin Earth concept can render a tabletop planetary/stellar system with selectable layers: terrain, astronomy data, weather/model data where relevant, settlements, missions, creator events, LIVE broadcasters, transport routes, educational markers and world portals. Selecting a destination can expand it from tabletop hologram into full-screen/AR/VR/MR world entry.

## Stubbs AI / Middleverse AI
AI guide functions: mission briefing, navigation, translation, accessibility assistance, contextual education, NPC orchestration, dynamic quest generation, world summaries and transition status. AI-generated claims in historical/scientific worlds require provenance/uncertainty handling and cannot silently overwrite curated source material.

## Accessibility
All time/planet experiences inherit seated mode, one-hand controls, voice navigation, keyboard/gamepad remapping, captions, transcripts, audio description, reduced motion, teleport locomotion, skip-transition control, adjustable travel intensity and non-VR equivalents.

## Creator economy
Creators can build original/licensed characters, missions, stages, spacecraft, settlements, clothing, props, educational exhibits and fictional timelines. Authorized assets use the Global Asset Library and Money Engine. Marketplace entitlements and creator/TRYAMM splits remain server-authoritative.

## Security and integrity
World transitions never bypass Quantum Security. Panic Mode can isolate a world/session, freeze privileged operations/money/device control, quarantine new content, preserve evidence and retain known-good checkpoints. Historical source packages and published world revisions are versioned so malicious or accidental edits can be rolled back.

## Release gate
Do not call the complete Time Worlds system GREEN until one vertical slice proves:
1. avatar enters a holographic portal;
2. Quantum Transition saves state;
3. destination world streams;
4. avatar/assets/progression restore;
5. another user can join realtime;
6. a mission can complete and persist;
7. an Echo/checkpoint can be revisited;
8. accessibility fallback works;
9. Panic Mode contains privileged operations without destroying the checkpoint;
10. the same destination works in standard screen mode plus at least one spatial mode (AR or VR/MR).

After that proof, Mars, Moon, historical worlds, Sheep Planet, stellar/constellation worlds and additional planets reuse the same runtime rather than becoming separate applications.

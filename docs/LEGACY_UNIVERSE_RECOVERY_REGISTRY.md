# TRYAMM Legacy Universe Recovery Registry

This registry preserves recovered system names and their intended roles so future app work does not depend on conversation recall alone.

## Personal + shared worlds
- My World — private/personal persistent universe for identity, media, assets, projects, purchases, creator history, settings, replays, game/world state and private memory.
- We Are the World — shared/global discovery and social universe for public or permissioned LIVE, events, communities, media, commerce and worlds across countries and languages.

- Metaverse — shared immersive creator/social world across web, mobile, TV, AR, VR and MR.
- Middleverse — bridge between real-world services, communications/call-center, AI guidance, commerce, media and immersive destinations.
- Middleverse AI — AI guide/orchestrator for service discovery, call-center assistance, translation, accessibility and navigation between experiences.
- Multiverse — federation of separate TRYAMM creator, game, media, simulation and community worlds with portable identity/state.
- Holoverse — holographic/spatial universe for AR/VR/MR, HoloForge assets, casting, spatial audio and immersive LIVE/media.
- Living Worlds — persistent game/social worlds with multiplayer state, missions, events, creator IP and HoloForge integration.
- Kingdom — community/faith/culture world using the shared backbone for events, media, education, commerce and immersive gatherings.
- New Yahisrale — community/faith/learning/media world using the same identity, security, LIVE, accessibility and global distribution systems.

# TRYAMM Free TV / Omni Box Network Distribution

## Goal
Provide a rights-aware, ad-supported and optionally subscription/transaction-supported TV and short-drama layer over HoloNet, Quantum Radio, Quantum Towers and conventional Internet/CDN connectivity.

## Distribution path
Content owner/licensor -> ingest/QC -> catalog/rights metadata -> transcoding/packaging -> Holo Edge/CDN -> Quantum Tower/local cache -> Quantum Radio/Quantum WiFi -> TV/tablet/phone/VR/AR/MR/casting client.

## Service modes
- FAST-style free ad-supported linear channels
- Free creator channels
- Omni Box short-drama/episode channels
- LIVE/PK event channels
- Sports/game/world event channels where rights permit
- Public-interest/community channels
- Premium subscription channels
- Transactional episode/movie unlocks
- Creator-owned storefront/video channels

## Rights and compliance gates
Every asset/channel must carry territory, window, device, monetization and redistribution rights metadata. HoloNet must refuse delivery when rights do not authorize the requested country/service/device/use. No copyrighted third-party channel or movie is redistributed merely because it is technically reachable.

## Advertising
Holo Advertise can select ads based on allowed context, audience preferences/consent and campaign constraints. Ads must not override accessibility settings, safety notices or age/regulated-content controls.

## Accessibility
- captions/subtitles
- transcripts
- audio description where available
- sign-language track/window where produced
- language/audio-track selection
- keyboard/voice/switch/one-hand navigation
- reduced motion in UI
- readable electronic program guide

## Network optimization
Quantum Lag Buster/Holo Edge can prioritize LIVE over bulk prefetch, pre-position popular licensed channels/episodes at Quantum Towers, adapt bitrate to measured congestion and preserve accessibility tracks during degradation.

## Proof metrics
- channel tune/startup time
- rebuffer ratio
- average delivered bitrate
- playback failure rate
- cache hit ratio
- ad start/failure rate
- LIVE glass-to-glass latency
- casting startup time
- caption/audio-description availability and sync
- concurrent viewers per edge/tower
- egress cost per viewing hour
- power per streamed hour on edge hardware

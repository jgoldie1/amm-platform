# Quantum Radio Network Profiles

## Profile A — HoloNet over public Internet
Uses conventional ISP/fiber/cable/5G backhaul with HoloNet identity, routing, edge caching and QoS above the transport.

## Profile B — Quantum WiFi / community edge
Quantum Radio and Quantum Towers provide local Wi-Fi/mesh/edge services with conventional upstream Internet connectivity.

## Profile C — 5G-ready integration
Uses certified 5G modem/small-cell/core components under valid operator or private-network authority. HoloNet runs as the application/service fabric above the mobile transport.

Required before activation:
- certified modem/radio hardware
- SIM/eSIM or private-core provisioning
- lawful spectrum/operator rights
- regional RF authorization
- emergency-service and lawful-intercept obligations where applicable
- carrier/core interoperability testing

## Profile D — 9G-Future research
Reserved architecture profile for future standards/research. The repository may model future capabilities, interfaces, traffic classes and test harnesses, but no public claim is made that 9G is standardized or deployed.

Research dimensions may include:
- extremely low-latency edge orchestration
- integrated sensing/communications
- AI-native network control
- high-frequency/optical research interfaces
- non-terrestrial/satellite integration
- post-quantum security
- QRNG-backed entropy
- immersive/holographic traffic classes

## Profile E — Quantum Tower federation
Multiple tower/edge sites federate through HoloNet, share signed service discovery, distribute caches/search shards, exchange telemetry and hand off authenticated sessions.

## Profile F — Free TV / Omni Box edge distribution
Quantum Towers cache rights-authorized channels/episodes, Quantum Lag Buster manages congestion, and Quantum Radio delivers to TVs, tablets, phones and XR clients.

## Benchmark dimensions
Across all profiles, the Benchmark & Proof Engine should record:
- latency P50/P95/P99
- jitter and packet loss
- handoff/roaming time
- session drop/recovery time
- throughput and concurrent devices
- LIVE dropped frames and PK sync drift
- VR/XR network latency
- casting startup
- Free TV startup/rebuffer/cache hit ratio
- accessibility-track preservation
- energy use and thermal throttling
- security/identity failures
- QRNG health where installed
